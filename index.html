Approximate tropical sky

A more personal sky

Calculate Sun, Moon, and rising sign with your birth time and coordinates. Enter the UTC offset that applied at birth, including daylight saving. This is a three-point sketch, not a full planetary or house chart.

North/east are positive; south/west are negative. Rising calculation supports latitudes from 65° south to 65° north.

Positions are approximate and may change sign near a boundary. Moon is geocentric. Rising depends strongly on accurate time, offset, and location. No rectification, houses, aspects, or planetary forecasts are calculated. Calculation reference.
